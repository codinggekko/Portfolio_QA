Exploratory Testing Report

Tesco Online Shop — Session 01

Testing type: Exploratory testing

Environment: Google Chrome, laptop/notebook

Customer type: Unregistered customer

Session status: Completed

1. Mission

Test making a small purchase on the Tesco Online Shop (Slovak version) with delivery.

URL address: https://potravinydomov.itesco.sk/shop/sk-SK/landing/groceries

2. Charter

  • Find one grocery product from the following categories: alcohol, bakery products, and fruit.
  • Add the selected product to the basket.
  • Set up a delivery time.

3. Charter vs. Opportunity

  • Only a product from the alcohol category was tested.
  • Adding the selected product to the basket could not be completed because registration was required.
  • Setting up a delivery time could not be tested because registration was required.

The registration requirement limited the scope of the original charter. The homepage already indicated “Sign in to start shopping”, and the test confirmed that registration was required to continue with the purchase.

4. Testing Approach

Manual exploratory testing was performed. During the session, the product search, filtering and sorting functionalities were evaluated, as well as the attempt to add a product to the basket.

Available products from the alcohol category were used as test data, including Pilsner beer products and products with different price values.

During the UX evaluation, particular attention was paid to the clarity of UI controls, the organisation and readability of the product catalogue, consistency of product information, and the ease of completing individual steps of the shopping journey.

8. Session Summary

The session focused on the Tesco Online Shop experience from the perspective of an unregistered customer. The exploration covered product discovery, category navigation, search, sorting, product information, UX consistency, and the attempt to add an alcoholic product to the basket.

Two functional defects related to price sorting were identified. Several UX/UI observations were also recorded, particularly concerning product ordering, inconsistent units, product-name presentation, and discoverability.

The original shopping journey could not be completed because registration was required when attempting to add the selected product to the basket. As a result, basket and delivery functionality should be investigated in a follow-up session using a registered customer account.
